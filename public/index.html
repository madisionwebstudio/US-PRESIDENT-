Meet Doug Jenkins

A soldier, a small-business owner, a grandfather, and a three-time candidate for President.

Doug Jenkins is a highly decorated Vietnam War veteran. He has been a sergeant and life coach since 1969, and he served 24 years in the United States Army.

Doug also ran his own successful painting business for almost 40 years.

He is a father of four, with ten grandchildren and two great-grandchildren.

Doug ran for President in 2020 and 2024. In 2028 he is running again as an independent write-in candidate, with a simple message: end the nonsense.

Doug has committed years of service, tirelessly working to improve our community.

Campaign priorities

The Platform

Ten areas of policy, from the cost of living to a new Department of Peace. Each has its own page with Doug’s full proposal.

  1. I Economy & Cost of LivingA one-year price freeze on rent, gas, groceries, medications and utilities.
  2. II Federal Budget ReformCut the federal budget and the national debt by 5% each, with a 10% flat income tax.
  3. III Stop the KillingEstablish a 16th federal department, the Department of Peace.
  4. IV Eradicate HomelessnessHousing as a right, and a family agent in all 58,000 fire stations.
  5. V Feed the Starving and Help the SickGive farmers, engineers and medical personnel the power to help those in need.
  6. VI Medical, Dental and Car CareAffordable coverage for all, Medicare for all, and car care similar to AAA.
  7. VII Prison and Criminal Justice ReformRehabilitation and education, with a plan to save taxpayers over $40 billion a year.
  8. VIII ImmigrationProcess immigrants in their home countries, prioritize much-needed workers, and partner with Mexico.
  9. IX Climate ChangeCommit resources to work with scientists and environmentalists worldwide.
  10. X AddictionEducation about the effects of addiction, with real treatment options.
