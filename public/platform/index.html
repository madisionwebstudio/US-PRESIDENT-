Campaign platform

The Platform

Ten policy commitments from Doug Jenkins’ 2028 campaign, from the cost of living and the federal budget to peace, homelessness and health care.

All policies

I

Economy & Cost of Living

A one-year price freeze on rent, gas, groceries, medications and utilities. Read the policy II

Federal Budget Reform

Cut the federal budget and the national debt by 5% each, with a 10% flat income tax. Read the policy III

Stop the Killing

Establish a 16th federal department, the Department of Peace. Read the policy IV

Eradicate Homelessness

Housing as a right, and a family agent in all 58,000 fire stations. Read the policy V

Feed the Starving and Help the Sick

Give farmers, engineers and medical personnel the power to help those in need. Read the policy VI

Medical, Dental and Car Care

Affordable coverage for all, Medicare for all, and car care similar to AAA. Read the policy VII

Prison and Criminal Justice Reform

Rehabilitation and education, with a plan to save taxpayers over $40 billion a year. Read the policy VIII

Immigration

Process immigrants in their home countries, prioritize much-needed workers, and partner with Mexico. Read the policy IX

Climate Change

Commit resources to work with scientists and environmentalists worldwide. Read the policy X

Addiction

Education about the effects of addiction, with real treatment options. Read the policy

Policy highlights

The plan at a glance

Specific, measurable proposals from across the platform.

  • 1 year Price freeze on rent, gas, groceries, medications and utilities Economy & Cost of Living
  • 10% Flat income tax Federal Budget Reform
  • 5% Cut to the federal budget by stopping careless and wasteful spending Federal Budget Reform
  • 5% Reduction in the national debt Federal Budget Reform
