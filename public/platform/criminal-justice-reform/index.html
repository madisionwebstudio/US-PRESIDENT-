Prison and Criminal Justice Reform

With around 4 million people incarcerated, Doug would refocus the system on rehabilitation and education, so they become more productive citizens.

Doug’s proposal

  1. Focus on rehabilitation and education for incarcerated people, so they become more productive citizens.
  2. Reform a prison system that is financially and morally unsustainable.
  3. Save taxpayers over $40 billion annually while creating safer and more just communities.
