Medical, Dental and Car Care

Doug supports affordable coverage for all Americans, from medical and dental care to mental health and even car care.

Doug’s proposal

  1. Affordable coverage for all.
  2. Medicare for all.
  3. Coverage for mental illness.
  4. Major drug reform laws.
  5. Car care similar to AAA coverage.

Why it matters

The campaign cites estimates that over 12% of Americans are depressed, and that many are taking illegal street drugs.

  • 12%+Of Americans estimated to be depressed
