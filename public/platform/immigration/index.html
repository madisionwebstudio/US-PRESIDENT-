Policy VIII of 10

Immigration

Doug calls for a more just immigration system built on real solutions, not “not in my back yard.”

Doug’s proposal

  1. Create a more just way to process immigrants in their home countries.
  2. Prioritize much-needed workers.
  3. Partner with Mexico in its security and economic growth.
  4. Create real solutions instead of NIMBY (“not in my back yard”) politics.
